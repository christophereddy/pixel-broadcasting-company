City desks

    Weather

    Forecasts for US cities come from the National Weather Service. The Paris desk uses Météo-France. PBC weather is not an official warning service; follow your local weather service during severe weather.

    Maps

    The story maps on the newsroom's video wall are drawn from Natural Earth and US Census Bureau outlines (both public domain), with city locations from GeoNames (CC BY 4.0). Borders are shown as Natural Earth draws them.

    SPORTS

    Each sport uses exactly one data source.

    • NFL, college football, NBA and WNBA: ESPN (scores, play-by-play, schedules, rosters, team logos and colors)
    • MLB: the MLB Stats API (© MLB Advanced Media, L.P.)
    • Ballpark dimensions: Wikipedia

    Team names, logos and marks belong to their owners. PBC is not affiliated with or endorsed by any league, team or data provider.
