ABOUT PBC

The Pixel Broadcasting Company is a pixel-art TV channel that never goes off the air.

Two channels share one screen. News is a pixel newsroom where a cast of reporters covers world, national, local, business, science and sports news and the weather. Sports animates real NFL, college football, NBA, WNBA and MLB games, play by play, as they happen.

ON THE DESK

The cast is fictional. The news and the games they cover are real.

  • Rosa Calloway and Marcus Hale, anchors
  • Evelyn Shaw, politics
  • Sunny Okafor, weather
  • Bo Kowalski, sports
  • Teo Marchetti, international field reporter
  • Jess Riley, local field reporter
  • Juniper Wilde, science, with Pip the axolotl
  • An unnamed black cat who wanders the anchor desk

Each sport on the Sports channel has its own two-person booth.

HOW IT'S MADE

News

Every three hours, an AI writing assistant (Claude, made by Anthropic) reads the latest reporting from our listed sources, picks the stories and writes the cast's lines. Every story is based on published reporting, and the source is credited on screen. Stories older than a day are dropped, so nothing stale stays on the air.

Sports

Games are drawn from live play-by-play data. The announcers' words are written for this channel, but every play they describe happened.

Mistakes

AI-written scripts can get things wrong. If you spot an error, our corrections page explains how to report it.

INDEPENDENCE

PBC is independent and owner-operated. Sponsors never choose or shape what we cover. Our Ad Policy explains how sponsorship works. To put your brand on the channel, visit the Marketing Division.
