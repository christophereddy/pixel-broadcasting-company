AD POLICY

Sponsors help keep the channel on the air. They never decide what's in the news.

Last updated October 6, 2026

HOW ADS APPEAR

  • Every ad lives inside the pixel world: arena boards, timeout screens, the News channel's commercial breaks and set pieces. We don't run outside video, pop-ups or banner networks.
  • Every ad is labeled, with SPONSOR, AD, or PRESENTED BY.
  • Ads are text only for now, set in the channel's pixel lettering. You approve exactly how they read before they air.
  • On-air wording is a fixed line you approve in advance. The cast reads it only in sponsor slots, never inside a news story.
  • No ad is ever styled as breaking news.

WHERE ADS NEVER APPEAR

  • Next to stories about deaths, disasters, violence or other tragedies
  • Inside the text of a news story or a weather warning

WHAT WE DON'T ACCEPT

  • Political or issue advertising
  • Tobacco, vaping, cannabis, alcohol or gambling
  • Weapons
  • Adult content
  • Payday loans, get-rich-quick schemes or speculative investments
  • Unproven health or medical claims
  • Anything misleading, hateful, or illegal where our viewers live

We may decline or remove any ad. If we remove a paid ad for reasons that aren't the advertiser's fault, we refund the unused time.

INDEPENDENCE

Sponsors have no say over which stories we cover or how. If a sponsor is in the news, we cover it the same way we would anyone else, and say on air that they sponsor the channel.

No viewer data is shared with advertisers. Sponsors receive overall audience totals only.
