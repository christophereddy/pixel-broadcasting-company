ACCESSIBILITY

The broadcast is pixel art, but the news is real text.

WHAT WORKS TODAY

  • Headlines and story summaries are on-screen text that screen readers announce as they change.
  • Sound is off by default. SOUND switches between off, short blips, and your device's own speech voice reading the stories.
  • Every button, menu and rundown item works with a keyboard and shows a gold focus outline.
  • The blinking ON AIR light stops if your device is set to reduce motion.
  • FULL SCREEN can be left with the Escape key.

KNOWN GAPS

  • The pixel animation itself (the newsroom set, the players' movements) has no text description.
  • Some small labels inside the broadcast are low contrast at phone sizes.
