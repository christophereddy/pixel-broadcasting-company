PRIVACY POLICY

Watching PBC doesn't require an account, and we don't build profiles of viewers.

Last updated October 6, 2026

WHAT THE CHANNEL STORES

We set no cookies. The page remembers two choices on your own device, in your browser's local storage: the city desk you picked, and the college football conference you picked. Clearing your browser's site data removes them.

Speech voices are your device's own. Nothing is recorded.

SERVICES YOUR BROWSER CONTACTS

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and may keep standard server logs, including IP addresses (GitHub's privacy statement).
  • Google Fonts supplies the pixel fonts.
  • The Sports channel loads live game data, logos and player photos directly from ESPN and MLB, which can see your IP address.

ANALYTICS

We don't run analytics yet. When we start counting viewers, we'll use a privacy-friendly service that sets no cookies and keeps no personal profiles, and we'll name it here before it's switched on.

FORMS AND EMAIL

If you send an advertising request or an email, we receive what you wrote and your contact details, through the form service named on that form once it opens. We use it only to answer you and run your ad. We don't sell it, and we never feed it to the AI that writes the news.

Payments, when they start, are handled by the payment provider. We never see or store card numbers.

CHILDREN

PBC is a general-audience channel. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

QUESTIONS OR REQUESTS

To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, write to . If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.
