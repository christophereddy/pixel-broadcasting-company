CORRECTIONS

When we get something wrong, we fix it and say so.

The news is rewritten every three hours, so a wrong story is usually corrected by the next refresh. For anything important, we fix it sooner.

REPORT AN ERROR

Email with the story's headline, roughly when you saw it, and what's wrong. A link to a reliable source helps.

Corrections are checked by a person against the original reporting. Messages are never read on air or passed to the AI that writes the news.
