Chicken Noodle Soup

Classic soup with chicken, vegetables and egg noodles.

Main · American

MEDIUM

AT A GLANCE

50 mintotal time
15 minprep
35 mincooking
6servings

Good for: comfort food, weeknight dinner.

Contains: Egg, Wheat.

INGREDIENTS

  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, sliced
  • 2 stalks celery, sliced
  • 1 lb chicken thighs (boneless, skinless)
  • 8 cups chicken stock
  • 3 sprigs thyme, sprig
  • 1 leaf bay leaf
  • 3 cups egg noodles
  • 2 tbsp parsley, chopped
  • Salt, to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste

METHOD

  1. Dice the onion and slice the carrots and celery.
  2. Cook the vegetables in the oil for 5 minutes.
    TIMER 5 MINMEDIUM HEAT
  3. Add the chicken, stock, thyme and bay leaf. Simmer for 20 minutes.
    TIMER 20 MINMEDIUM-LOW HEAT
  4. Lift out the chicken and check it reads 165°F, then shred it with two forks.Done when: shreds easily.
    CHECK 165°F / 74°C INSIDE
  5. Add the noodles and cook for 7 minutes.
    TIMER 7 MINMEDIUM HEAT
  6. Return the chicken, take out the bay leaf and thyme stems, and season.
  7. Top with parsley.
