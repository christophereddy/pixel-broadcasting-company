Spaghetti and Meatballs

Tender meatballs simmered in tomato sauce over spaghetti.

Main · Italian-American

MEDIUM

AT A GLANCE

1 hrtotal time
20 minprep
40 mincooking
4servings

Good for: family dinner, comfort food.

Contains: Milk, Egg, Wheat.

INGREDIENTS

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • ½ cup breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • ¾ cup parmesan, grated
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp parsley, chopped
  • 1½ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 can canned crushed tomatoes (28 oz can)
  • 6 leaves basil, torn
  • 1 lb spaghetti
  • 16 cups water

METHOD

  1. Mince 2 cloves of garlic for the meatballs and the last one for the sauce. Dice the onion.
  2. Mix the beef, breadcrumbs, egg, 1/2 cup parmesan, parsley, 1 tsp salt and the pepper. Roll into 16 balls.
  3. Brown the meatballs in the oil for 6 minutes, turning, then lift them out.Done when: browned all over.
    TIMER 6 MINMEDIUM-HIGH HEAT
  4. Cook the onion in the same pan for 4 minutes.
    TIMER 4 MINMEDIUM HEAT
  5. Add the tomatoes, return the meatballs, cover and simmer for 20 minutes.
    TIMER 20 MINLOW HEAT
  6. Check a meatball: 160°F inside.Done when: no pink in the middle.
    LOW HEATCHECK 160°F / 71°C INSIDE
  7. Meanwhile, boil the water with the rest of the salt and cook the spaghetti for 9 minutes. Drain.Done when: al dente.
    TIMER 9 MINHIGH HEAT
  8. Stir the basil into the sauce and serve over the spaghetti with the rest of the parmesan.
