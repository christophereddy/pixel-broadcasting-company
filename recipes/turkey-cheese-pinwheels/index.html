Turkey and Cheese Pinwheels

Rolled-up tortilla spirals for the lunchbox.

Main · American

EASYKID-FRIENDLYQUICK

AT A GLANCE

10 mintotal time
10 minprep
0 mincooking
2servings

Good for: lunchbox, snack, picnic.

Contains: Milk, Wheat.

INGREDIENTS

  • 2 flour tortillas
  • 4 tbsp cream cheese, softened
  • 6 slices sliced deli turkey, sliced
  • 2 slices cheddar, sliced
  • 1 cup spinach

METHOD

  1. Spread the cream cheese all over each tortilla.
  2. Layer turkey, cheddar and spinach on top.
  3. Roll each tortilla up tightly.
  4. Cut each roll into 6 slices.
