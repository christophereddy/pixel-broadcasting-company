Cumin Rice

Fluffy basmati-style rice toasted with cumin seeds.

Side · India

EASYVEGETARIAN

AT A GLANCE

30 mintotal time
5 minprep
20 mincooking
4servings

Good for: weeknight dinner, family dinner.

Contains: Milk.

INGREDIENTS

  • 1½ cups rice, rinsed
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 tsp cumin (seeds)
  • 2¼ cups water
  • ¾ tsp salt

METHOD

  1. Rinse the rice until the water runs clear.
  2. Melt the butter and fry the cumin seeds for 1 minute.Done when: fragrant.
    TIMER 1 MINMEDIUM HEAT
  3. Add the rice, water and salt and bring to a boil.
    HIGH HEAT
  4. Cover and cook for 15 minutes.
    TIMER 15 MINLOW HEAT
  5. Rest for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork.
    TIMER 5 MIN
