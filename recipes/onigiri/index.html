Tuna Mayo Onigiri

Rice balls with a tuna mayo filling, wrapped in nori.

Main · Japan

EASYKID-FRIENDLY

AT A GLANCE

50 mintotal time
15 minprep
35 mincooking
6servings

Good for: lunchbox, picnic.

Contains: Egg, Fish.

INGREDIENTS

  • 2 cups short-grain rice
  • 2¼ cups water
  • 1 can canned tuna, drained
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 3 sheets nori sheets (cut in half)

METHOD

  1. Rinse the rice until the water runs clear.
  2. Cook the rice in the water, covered, for 15 minutes.
    TIMER 15 MINLOW HEATGROWN-UP HELPS
  3. Rest it, still covered, for 10 minutes.Done when: soft and sticky.
    TIMER 10 MIN
  4. Mix the tuna with the mayonnaise.
  5. Wet your hands, rub on a little salt and press a handful of rice flat.
  6. Put a spoon of tuna in the middle, close the rice around it and press into a triangle.
  7. Wrap the bottom in a strip of nori.
