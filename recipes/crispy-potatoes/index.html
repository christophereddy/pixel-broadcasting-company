Crispy Roast Potatoes

Potatoes roasted until crunchy with rosemary.

Side · American

EASYVEGETARIAN

AT A GLANCE

55 mintotal time
10 minprep
45 mincooking
4servings

Good for: family dinner.

Contains: none of the major allergens.

INGREDIENTS

  • 2 lb potatoes, cubed
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 sprigs rosemary, chopped

METHOD

  1. Preheat the oven to 425°F.
    OVEN 425°F / 218°C
  2. Cut the potatoes into 1 inch chunks.
  3. Toss with the oil, salt and rosemary on a sheet pan.
  4. Roast for 45 minutes, turning halfway.Done when: crisp and golden.
    TIMER 45 MINOVEN 425°F / 218°C
