Fluffy Pancakes

Tall, soft pancakes with maple syrup.

Breakfast · American

EASYKID-FRIENDLYVEGETARIAN

AT A GLANCE

25 mintotal time
10 minprep
15 mincooking
4servings

Good for: breakfast, brunch.

Contains: Milk, Egg, Wheat.

INGREDIENTS

  • 1½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1¼ cups milk
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 4 tbsp butter, melted
  • ½ cup maple syrup

METHOD

  1. Whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder and salt in a big bowl.
  2. In another bowl, whisk the milk, egg and 3 tbsp of the melted butter.
  3. Pour the wet into the dry and stir until just mixed. Small lumps are fine.
  4. Brush a pan with the last of the butter. Pour in 1/4 cup of batter per pancake.
    MEDIUM HEATGROWN-UP HELPS
  5. Cook for 2 minutes, until bubbles pop on top.Done when: bubbles on top.
    TIMER 2 MINMEDIUM HEATGROWN-UP HELPS
  6. Flip and cook 1 minute more, until golden.Done when: golden both sides.
    TIMER 1 MINMEDIUM HEATGROWN-UP HELPS
  7. Serve warm with maple syrup.
