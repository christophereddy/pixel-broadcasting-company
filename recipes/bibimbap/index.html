Bibimbap

Rice bowl with seasoned beef, vegetables, a fried egg and gochujang.

Main · South Korea

MEDIUM

AT A GLANCE

55 mintotal time
25 minprep
30 mincooking
4servings

Good for: weeknight dinner, family dinner.

Contains: Egg, Wheat, Soy, Sesame.

INGREDIENTS

  • 1½ cups rice, rinsed
  • 2¼ cups water
  • ½ lb ground beef
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp sesame oil
  • 4 cups spinach
  • 2 carrots, shredded
  • 1 zucchini, sliced
  • 2 cups bean sprouts
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 4 eggs
  • 4 tbsp gochujang
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds, toasted

METHOD

  1. Cook the rice in the water, covered, for 15 minutes, then rest it.
    TIMER 15 MINLOW HEAT
  2. Mince the garlic, shred the carrots and slice the zucchini.
  3. Cook the beef with the soy sauce, sugar and garlic for 6 minutes, breaking it up. It should reach 160°F.Done when: browned, no pink.
    TIMER 6 MINMEDIUM-HIGH HEATCHECK 160°F / 71°C INSIDE
  4. Stir-fry each vegetable in a little vegetable oil for 2 minutes, one at a time.
    TIMER 2 MINMEDIUM-HIGH HEAT
  5. Fry the eggs in the rest of the oil for 3 minutes.Done when: whites set, yolk runny.
    TIMER 3 MINMEDIUM HEAT
  6. Fill bowls with rice, arrange the beef and vegetables on top, then the egg.
  7. Add gochujang, a drizzle of sesame oil and sesame seeds.
