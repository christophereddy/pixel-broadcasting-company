Miso Soup

Light dashi broth with miso, tofu and seaweed.

Starter · Japan

EASYKID-FRIENDLYQUICK

AT A GLANCE

15 mintotal time
5 minprep
10 mincooking
4servings

Good for: lunch, lunchbox.

Contains: Soy, Fish.

INGREDIENTS

  • 4 cups dashi stock
  • 1 tbsp dried wakame seaweed
  • 7 oz firm tofu, cubed
  • 3 tbsp white miso
  • 2 green onions, sliced

METHOD

  1. Soak the wakame in water for 5 minutes, then drain.
    TIMER 5 MIN
  2. Cut the tofu into small cubes and slice the green onions.
    GROWN-UP HELPS
  3. Heat the dashi until steaming.
    MEDIUM HEATGROWN-UP HELPS
  4. Add the tofu and wakame for 2 minutes.
    TIMER 2 MINLOW HEATGROWN-UP HELPS
  5. Whisk the miso with a ladle of broth, then stir it back in. Don't let it boil.
    LOW HEATGROWN-UP HELPS
  6. Top with green onion.
