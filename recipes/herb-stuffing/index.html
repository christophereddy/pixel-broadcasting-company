Herb Stuffing

Crisp-topped bread stuffing with celery, onion and sage.

Side · American

MEDIUMVEGETARIAN

AT A GLANCE

1 hr 5 mintotal time
20 minprep
45 mincooking
8servings

Good for: holiday.

Contains: Milk, Egg, Wheat.

INGREDIENTS

  • 12 slices sandwich bread, cubed (day-old)
  • 6 tbsp butter
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 stalks celery, diced
  • 8 leaves sage, chopped
  • 3 sprigs thyme, chopped
  • 2 cups vegetable stock
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 tsp salt

METHOD

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F and butter a baking dish.
    OVEN 350°F / 177°C
  2. Cube the bread and dice the onion and celery.
  3. Cook the onion and celery in the rest of the butter for 8 minutes.Done when: soft.
    TIMER 8 MINMEDIUM HEAT
  4. Stir in the sage, thyme and salt.
  5. Toss with the bread, stock and egg, and spread in the dish.
  6. Cover and bake for 25 minutes.
    TIMER 25 MINOVEN 350°F / 177°C
  7. Uncover and bake 20 minutes more, until crisp on top and 165°F in the middle.Done when: crisp top.
    TIMER 20 MINOVEN 350°F / 177°CCHECK 165°F / 74°C INSIDE
