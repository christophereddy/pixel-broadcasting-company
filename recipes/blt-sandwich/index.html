BLT

Crispy bacon, crunchy lettuce and ripe tomato on toast.

Main · American

EASYQUICK

AT A GLANCE

15 mintotal time
5 minprep
10 mincooking
2servings

Good for: lunch.

Contains: Egg, Wheat.

INGREDIENTS

  • 6 slices bacon
  • 4 slices sandwich bread, toasted
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 4 leaves lettuce
  • 1 tomato, sliced

METHOD

  1. Cook the bacon in a pan for 8 minutes, turning now and then, until crisp.Done when: crisp and browned.
    TIMER 8 MINMEDIUM HEAT
  2. Drain the bacon on a paper towel. Toast the bread meanwhile.
  3. Slice the tomato.
  4. Spread mayonnaise on the toast.
  5. Layer lettuce, tomato and 3 slices of bacon on 2 slices of toast, top with the rest and cut in half.
