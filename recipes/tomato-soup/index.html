Creamy Tomato Soup

A smooth, gently creamy tomato soup for dunking.

Starter · American

EASYKID-FRIENDLYVEGETARIAN

AT A GLANCE

35 mintotal time
10 minprep
25 mincooking
4servings

Good for: lunch, comfort food.

Contains: Milk.

INGREDIENTS

  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 cans canned crushed tomatoes (28 oz cans)
  • 2 cups vegetable stock
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • ½ cup heavy cream
  • 4 leaves basil, torn
  • Salt, to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste

METHOD

  1. Chop the onion and mince the garlic.
    GROWN-UP HELPS
  2. Melt the butter in a pot and cook the onion for 5 minutes, until soft.Done when: onion soft.
    TIMER 5 MINMEDIUM HEATGROWN-UP HELPS
  3. Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute.
    TIMER 1 MINMEDIUM HEATGROWN-UP HELPS
  4. Add the tomatoes, stock and sugar. Simmer for 15 minutes.
    TIMER 15 MINMEDIUM-LOW HEATGROWN-UP HELPS
  5. Blend until smooth. A grown-up holds the lid down with a towel.
    GROWN-UP HELPS
  6. Stir in the cream and basil, then season with salt and pepper.
    LOW HEATGROWN-UP HELPS
