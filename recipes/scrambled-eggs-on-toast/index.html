Soft Scrambled Eggs on Toast

Slow, creamy scrambled eggs on buttered toast.

Breakfast · American

EASYQUICKVEGETARIAN

AT A GLANCE

10 mintotal time
2 minprep
8 mincooking
2servings

Good for: breakfast.

Contains: Milk, Egg, Wheat.

INGREDIENTS

  • 4 eggs, beaten
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 slices sandwich bread, toasted
  • 1 tbsp chives, chopped
  • Salt, to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste

METHOD

  1. Beat the eggs with a pinch of salt.
  2. Melt 1 tbsp butter in a pan over low heat and pour in the eggs.
    LOW HEAT
  3. Stir slowly for 5 minutes, until softly set.Done when: softly set, no runny egg.
    TIMER 5 MINLOW HEAT
  4. Toast the bread and butter it with the rest of the butter.
  5. Spoon the eggs onto the toast and top with chives and pepper.
