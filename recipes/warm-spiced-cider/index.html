Warm Spiced Cider

Apple cider simmered with cinnamon, cloves and orange.

Drink · American

EASYKID-FRIENDLYVEGETARIAN

AT A GLANCE

20 mintotal time
5 minprep
15 mincooking
6servings

Good for: holiday, party.

Contains: none of the major allergens.

INGREDIENTS

  • 6 cups apple cider
  • 2 cinnamons (sticks)
  • 6 cloves
  • 1 orange, sliced

METHOD

  1. Slice the orange.
    GROWN-UP HELPS
  2. Put the cider, cinnamon sticks, cloves and orange in a pot.
  3. Simmer for 15 minutes.Done when: steaming and fragrant.
    TIMER 15 MINLOW HEATGROWN-UP HELPS
  4. Ladle into mugs, leaving the spices behind.
    LOW HEATGROWN-UP HELPS
