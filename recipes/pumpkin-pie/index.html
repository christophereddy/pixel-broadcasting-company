Pumpkin Pie

Silky spiced pumpkin pie.

Dessert · American

MEDIUMVEGETARIAN

AT A GLANCE

3 hr 10 mintotal time
15 minprep
55 mincooking
8servings

Good for: holiday, baking.

Contains: Milk, Egg, Wheat.

INGREDIENTS

  • 1 pie crust (unbaked 9 inch crust)
  • 15 oz pumpkin purée
  • 12 oz evaporated milk
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • ¾ cup brown sugar
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • ½ tsp ground ginger
  • ¼ tsp nutmeg
  • ½ tsp salt

METHOD

  1. Preheat the oven to 425°F and set the crust in a pie dish.
    OVEN 425°F / 218°C
  2. Whisk the pumpkin, evaporated milk, eggs, brown sugar, spices and salt.
  3. Pour into the crust and bake for 15 minutes.
    TIMER 15 MINOVEN 425°F / 218°C
  4. Lower the oven to 350°F and bake 40 minutes more.Done when: edges set, centre barely wobbles.
    TIMER 40 MINOVEN 350°F / 177°C
  5. Cool for 2 hours before slicing.
    TIMER 2 HR
