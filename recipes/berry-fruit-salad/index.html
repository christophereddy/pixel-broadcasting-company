Berry Fruit Salad

Bright berries with honey, lime and mint.

Side · American

EASYKID-FRIENDLYQUICKVEGETARIAN

AT A GLANCE

10 mintotal time
10 minprep
0 mincooking
4servings

Good for: breakfast, brunch, picnic.

Contains: none of the major allergens.

INGREDIENTS

  • 2 cups strawberries, sliced
  • 1 cup blueberries
  • 1 cup raspberries
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • 6 leaves mint, chopped

METHOD

  1. Slice the strawberries.
    GROWN-UP HELPS
  2. Put the strawberries, blueberries and raspberries in a bowl.
  3. Squeeze the lime and stir the juice with the honey.
  4. Pour over the berries, add the mint and toss gently.
