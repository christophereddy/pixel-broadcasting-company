Ants on a Log

Celery, peanut butter and raisin 'ants'.

Snack · American

EASYKID-FRIENDLYQUICKVEGETARIAN

AT A GLANCE

5 mintotal time
5 minprep
0 mincooking
2servings

Good for: lunchbox, snack.

Contains: Peanuts.

INGREDIENTS

  • 3 stalks celery
  • 3 tbsp peanut butter
  • 2 tbsp raisins

METHOD

  1. Cut each celery stalk into 3 pieces.
    GROWN-UP HELPS
  2. Fill the groove of each piece with peanut butter.
  3. Line raisins along the top.
