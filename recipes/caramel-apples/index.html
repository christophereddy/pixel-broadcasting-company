Caramel Apples

Crisp apples dipped in homemade caramel.

Dessert · American

MEDIUMKID-FRIENDLYVEGETARIAN

AT A GLANCE

1 hrtotal time
10 minprep
15 mincooking
6servings

Good for: party, holiday.

Contains: Milk, Peanuts.

INGREDIENTS

  • 6 apples (small, with sticks)
  • 1 cup sugar
  • ¼ cup water
  • 4 tbsp butter
  • ½ cup heavy cream, warm
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ½ cup peanuts, chopped

METHOD

  1. Push a stick into the top of each apple and line a tray with parchment.
  2. Chop the peanuts.
    GROWN-UP HELPS
  3. Cook the sugar and water without stirring for 10 minutes, until amber. A grown-up does this part: it's very hot.Done when: deep amber.
    TIMER 10 MINMEDIUM HEATGROWN-UP HELPS
  4. Off the heat, whisk in the butter, then the warm cream, vanilla and salt.
    LOW HEATGROWN-UP HELPS
  5. Cool the caramel for 5 minutes.
    TIMER 5 MIN
  6. Dip each apple, let it drip, then roll the bottom in peanuts.
  7. Set on the tray for 30 minutes.Done when: caramel set.
    TIMER 30 MIN
