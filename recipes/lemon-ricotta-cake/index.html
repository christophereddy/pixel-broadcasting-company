Lemon Ricotta Cake

A moist, tender cake with ricotta and lemon.

Dessert · Italy

MEDIUMVEGETARIAN

AT A GLANCE

1 hr 15 mintotal time
15 minprep
40 mincooking
8servings

Good for: baking, family dinner.

Contains: Milk, Egg, Wheat.

INGREDIENTS

  • ½ cup butter, softened
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 cup ricotta
  • 1 lemon, zested (zest and juice)
  • 1½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp powdered sugar, sifted

METHOD

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F and butter a 9 inch round pan.
    OVEN 350°F / 177°C
  2. Beat the butter and sugar until fluffy, then beat in the eggs one at a time.
  3. Zest and juice the lemon and beat it in with the ricotta.
  4. Fold in the flour, baking powder and salt.
  5. Bake for 40 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean.Done when: golden, toothpick clean.
    TIMER 40 MINOVEN 350°F / 177°C
  6. Cool for 20 minutes, then dust with powdered sugar.
    TIMER 20 MIN
