Sweet Crêpes

Thin crêpes with sugar and lemon or jam.

Dessert · France

MEDIUMKID-FRIENDLYVEGETARIAN

AT A GLANCE

35 mintotal time
10 minprep
20 mincooking
4servings

Good for: brunch, breakfast.

Contains: Milk, Egg, Wheat.

INGREDIENTS

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 1¼ cups milk
  • 3 tbsp butter, melted
  • 3 tbsp sugar
  • ½ tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • ¼ cup strawberry jam

METHOD

  1. Whisk the flour, eggs, milk, 2 tbsp butter, 1 tbsp sugar and vanilla until smooth.
  2. Rest the batter for 10 minutes.
    TIMER 10 MIN
  3. Brush a pan with the rest of the butter and pour in 1/4 cup of batter, tilting to spread it thin.
    MEDIUM HEATGROWN-UP HELPS
  4. Cook for 1 minute, flip and cook 30 seconds more.Done when: lacy golden spots.
    TIMER 1 MINMEDIUM HEATGROWN-UP HELPS
  5. Fill with sugar and a squeeze of lemon, or with jam, and fold into quarters.
