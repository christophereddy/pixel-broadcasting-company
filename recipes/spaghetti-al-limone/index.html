Spaghetti al Limone

Spaghetti in a silky lemon, butter and parmesan sauce.

Main · Italy

MEDIUMVEGETARIAN

AT A GLANCE

20 mintotal time
5 minprep
15 mincooking
4servings

Good for: weeknight dinner, date night.

Contains: Milk, Wheat.

INGREDIENTS

  • 1 lb spaghetti
  • 16 cups water
  • 1 tbsp salt
  • 4 tbsp butter
  • 2 lemons, zested (zest and juice)
  • 1 cup parmesan, grated
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • ½ tsp black pepper

METHOD

  1. Boil the water with the salt and cook the spaghetti for 9 minutes.Done when: al dente.
    TIMER 9 MINHIGH HEAT
  2. Zest and juice the lemons.
  3. Save 1 cup of pasta water, then drain.
    HIGH HEAT
  4. Melt the butter with the oil in the pot, add the lemon and a splash of pasta water.
    LOW HEAT
  5. Toss in the spaghetti and parmesan for 2 minutes, adding pasta water until glossy.Done when: glossy sauce.
    TIMER 2 MINLOW HEAT
  6. Finish with black pepper.
