Caesar Salad

Crisp romaine with a creamy garlic-parmesan dressing and croutons.

Starter · American

MEDIUM

AT A GLANCE

25 mintotal time
15 minprep
10 mincooking
2servings

Good for: date night, lunch.

Contains: Milk, Egg, Wheat, Fish.

INGREDIENTS

  • 1 romaine, torn (1 head)
  • 2 slices sandwich bread, cubed
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • ½ lemon, juiced
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp dijon mustard
  • 2 anchovy fillets, minced
  • ½ cup parmesan, grated
  • Black pepper, to taste

METHOD

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F.
    OVEN 375°F / 191°C
  2. Cube the bread and toss it with 2 tbsp olive oil.
  3. Bake the cubes for 10 minutes, until crunchy.Done when: golden croutons.
    TIMER 10 MINOVEN 375°F / 191°C
  4. Mince the garlic and anchovies, then whisk them with the mayonnaise, lemon, mustard, the rest of the oil and half the parmesan.
  5. Toss the romaine with the dressing and croutons.
  6. Top with the rest of the parmesan and pepper.
