Homemade Pizza

Make the dough, then everyone tops their own.

Main · Italian-American

MEDIUMKID-FRIENDLY

AT A GLANCE

1 hr 30 mintotal time
20 minprep
15 mincooking
4servings

Good for: party, family dinner.

Contains: Milk, Wheat.

INGREDIENTS

  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2¼ tsp instant yeast
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1½ tsp salt
  • 1 cup water, warm
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 cup canned crushed tomatoes
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 2 cups mozzarella, shredded
  • 24 slices pepperoni, sliced
  • 6 leaves basil, torn

METHOD

  1. Mix the flour, yeast, sugar and 1 tsp salt. Stir in the warm water and 2 tbsp oil.
  2. Knead for 5 minutes, until smooth.Done when: smooth and stretchy.
    TIMER 5 MIN
  3. Cover and let it rise for 1 hour.Done when: doubled in size.
    TIMER 1 HR
  4. Preheat the oven to 475°F. Stir the oregano and the rest of the salt into the tomatoes.
    OVEN 475°F / 246°CGROWN-UP HELPS
  5. Split the dough in 2 and stretch each into a round on an oiled sheet.
  6. Spread the sauce, then add mozzarella and pepperoni.
  7. Bake for 12 minutes, until the crust is golden and the cheese bubbles.Done when: bubbling cheese.
    TIMER 12 MINOVEN 475°F / 246°CGROWN-UP HELPS
  8. Top with basil and cut into slices.
    GROWN-UP HELPS
