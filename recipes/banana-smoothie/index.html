Banana Smoothie

Thick, creamy banana smoothie.

Drink · American

EASYKID-FRIENDLYQUICKVEGETARIAN

AT A GLANCE

5 mintotal time
5 minprep
0 mincooking
2servings

Good for: breakfast, snack.

Contains: Milk.

INGREDIENTS

  • 2 bananas, sliced
  • 1 cup milk, cold
  • ½ cup plain yogurt, cold
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 cup ice cubes

METHOD

  1. Peel the bananas and break them into chunks.
  2. Put the bananas, milk, yogurt, honey and ice in the blender.
  3. Blend until smooth and pour into glasses.
