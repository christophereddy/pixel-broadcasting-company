Skillet Cornbread

Golden, slightly sweet cornbread.

Side · American

EASYVEGETARIAN

AT A GLANCE

32 mintotal time
10 minprep
22 mincooking
8servings

Good for: game day, family dinner.

Contains: Milk, Egg, Wheat.

INGREDIENTS

  • 1 cup cornmeal
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • ¼ cup sugar
  • 1 tbsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 6 tbsp butter, melted

METHOD

  1. Preheat the oven to 400°F with a buttered skillet inside.
    OVEN 400°F / 204°C
  2. Whisk the cornmeal, flour, sugar, baking powder and salt.
  3. Whisk in the milk, eggs and the rest of the melted butter.
  4. Pour into the hot skillet and bake for 22 minutes.Done when: golden, springs back.
    TIMER 22 MINOVEN 400°F / 204°C
  5. Cut into wedges.
