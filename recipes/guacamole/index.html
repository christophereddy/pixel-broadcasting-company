Guacamole

Chunky avocado dip with lime and cilantro.

Side · Mexican

EASYQUICKVEGETARIAN

AT A GLANCE

10 mintotal time
10 minprep
0 mincooking
4servings

Good for: party, snack, weeknight dinner.

Contains: none of the major allergens.

INGREDIENTS

  • 3 avocadoes, mashed
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • ¼ cup red onion, diced
  • 1 tomato, diced
  • 2 tbsp cilantro, chopped
  • 1 jalapeno, minced (seeds removed)
  • ½ tsp salt

METHOD

  1. Dice the onion and tomato and mince the jalapeño.
  2. Halve the avocados, scoop them into a bowl and mash with a fork.
  3. Stir in the lime juice and salt.
  4. Fold in the onion, tomato, jalapeño and cilantro.
