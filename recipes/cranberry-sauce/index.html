Cranberry Sauce

Fresh cranberries cooked down with orange.

Sauce · American

EASYVEGETARIAN

AT A GLANCE

20 mintotal time
5 minprep
12 mincooking
8servings

Good for: holiday.

Contains: none of the major allergens.

INGREDIENTS

  • 12 oz cranberries
  • ¾ cup sugar
  • 1 orange, juiced
  • ½ cup water

METHOD

  1. Zest and juice the orange.
  2. Put the cranberries, sugar, orange and water in a pot.
  3. Simmer for 12 minutes, until most berries pop.Done when: berries burst, sauce thick.
    TIMER 12 MINMEDIUM HEAT
  4. Cool. It thickens as it cools.
