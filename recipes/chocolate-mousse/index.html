Chocolate Mousse

Light, rich chocolate mousse made with whipped cream.

Dessert · French

MEDIUMVEGETARIAN

AT A GLANCE

2 hr 20 mintotal time
15 minprep
5 mincooking
4servings

Good for: date night, party.

Contains: Milk.

INGREDIENTS

  • 6 oz dark chocolate, chopped
  • 1½ cups heavy cream, cold
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 pinch salt

METHOD

  1. Chop the chocolate.
  2. Warm 1/2 cup cream until steaming, about 3 minutes.Done when: steaming, not boiling.
    TIMER 3 MINMEDIUM-LOW HEAT
  3. Pour it over the chocolate, wait 1 minute, then stir until smooth.
    TIMER 1 MIN
  4. Whip the rest of the cream with the sugar, vanilla and salt to soft peaks.Done when: soft peaks.
  5. Fold the whipped cream into the chocolate in 3 parts.
  6. Spoon into glasses and chill for 2 hours.Done when: set.
    TIMER 2 HR
