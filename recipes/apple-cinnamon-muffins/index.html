Apple Cinnamon Muffins

Soft muffins full of apple and cinnamon.

Breakfast · American

EASYKID-FRIENDLYVEGETARIAN

AT A GLANCE

40 mintotal time
15 minprep
22 mincooking
12servings

Good for: baking, breakfast, snack.

Contains: Milk, Egg, Wheat.

INGREDIENTS

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • ½ cup sugar
  • ¼ cup brown sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 2 tsp cinnamon
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • ¾ cup milk
  • ½ cup butter, melted
  • 2 apples, diced

METHOD

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F and line a 12-cup muffin tin.
    OVEN 375°F / 191°CGROWN-UP HELPS
  2. Peel and dice the apples.
    GROWN-UP HELPS
  3. Whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder, salt and 1 1/2 tsp cinnamon.
  4. Whisk the eggs, milk and butter, then stir them into the flour until just mixed.
  5. Fold in the apples and fill the cups.
  6. Mix the brown sugar with the rest of the cinnamon and sprinkle on top.
  7. Bake for 22 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean.Done when: toothpick comes out clean.
