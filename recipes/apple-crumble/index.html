Apple Crumble

Soft cinnamon apples under a crunchy oat topping.

Dessert · British

EASYVEGETARIAN

AT A GLANCE

1 hrtotal time
20 minprep
40 mincooking
6servings

Good for: family dinner, baking.

Contains: Milk, Wheat.

INGREDIENTS

  • 6 apples, sliced
  • ½ lemon, juiced
  • ¼ cup sugar
  • 1½ tsp cinnamon
  • ¾ cup all-purpose flour
  • ¾ cup rolled oats
  • ½ cup brown sugar
  • 6 tbsp butter, cold

METHOD

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F.
    OVEN 375°F / 191°C
  2. Peel and slice the apples. Toss with the lemon, sugar and 1 tsp cinnamon in a baking dish.
  3. Rub the flour, oats, brown sugar, the rest of the cinnamon and the cold butter together into crumbs.
  4. Scatter the crumbs over the apples.
  5. Bake for 40 minutes, until bubbling and golden.Done when: bubbling at the edges.
    TIMER 40 MINOVEN 375°F / 191°C
