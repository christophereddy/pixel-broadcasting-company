Grilled Cheese

Golden, buttery bread around melted cheddar.

Main · American

EASYKID-FRIENDLYVEGETARIAN

AT A GLANCE

15 mintotal time
5 minprep
8 mincooking
2servings

Good for: lunch, comfort food.

Contains: Milk, Wheat.

INGREDIENTS

  • 4 slices sandwich bread
  • 2 tbsp butter, softened
  • 4 slices cheddar, sliced

METHOD

  1. Spread butter on one side of every slice of bread.
    GROWN-UP HELPS
  2. Lay 2 slices butter side down and put 2 slices of cheddar on each. Top with the other bread, butter side up.
  3. Cook in a pan for 4 minutes, until the bottom is golden.Done when: golden underneath.
    TIMER 4 MINMEDIUM HEATGROWN-UP HELPS
  4. Flip with a spatula and cook the other side for 4 minutes, until the cheese melts.Done when: cheese melted.
    TIMER 4 MINMEDIUM HEATGROWN-UP HELPS
  5. Cut each sandwich in half.
    GROWN-UP HELPS
