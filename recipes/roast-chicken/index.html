Lemon Herb Roast Chicken

A whole chicken roasted with lemon, garlic and thyme.

Main · American

MEDIUM

AT A GLANCE

1 hr 40 mintotal time
15 minprep
1 hr 15 mincooking
4servings

Good for: family dinner.

Contains: Milk.

INGREDIENTS

  • 4 lb whole chicken
  • 3 tbsp butter, softened
  • 1 lemon
  • 6 cloves garlic
  • 6 sprigs thyme, sprig
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper

METHOD

  1. Preheat the oven to 425°F. Pat the chicken dry.
    OVEN 425°F / 218°C
  2. Rub the butter all over and season with salt and pepper.
  3. Halve the lemon and put it inside with the garlic and thyme.
  4. Roast for 75 minutes.Done when: skin deep golden.
    TIMER 75 MINOVEN 425°F / 218°C
  5. Check the thickest part of the thigh: 165°F.
    OVEN 425°F / 218°CCHECK 165°F / 74°C INSIDE
  6. Rest for 10 minutes before carving.
    TIMER 10 MIN
