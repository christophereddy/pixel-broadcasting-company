Mashed Potatoes

Smooth, buttery mashed potatoes.

Side · American

EASYVEGETARIAN

AT A GLANCE

30 mintotal time
10 minprep
20 mincooking
4servings

Good for: date night, family dinner, holiday.

Contains: Milk.

INGREDIENTS

  • 2 lb potatoes, cubed
  • 10 cups water
  • 4 tbsp butter
  • ¾ cup milk, warm
  • 1½ tsp salt

METHOD

  1. Peel and cube the potatoes.
  2. Cover with the water, add 1 tsp salt and boil for 15 minutes.Done when: a knife slides in easily.
    TIMER 15 MINHIGH HEAT
  3. Drain and mash with the butter.
  4. Stir in the warm milk and the rest of the salt.
