Chana Masala

Chickpeas simmered in a spiced tomato and onion gravy.

Main · India

MEDIUMVEGETARIAN

AT A GLANCE

50 mintotal time
15 minprep
35 mincooking
4servings

Good for: weeknight dinner, family dinner.

Contains: none of the major allergens.

INGREDIENTS

  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 2 tsp coriander
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 1½ cups canned crushed tomatoes
  • 2 cans canned chickpeas, drained (15 oz cans)
  • ½ cup water
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ lemon, juiced
  • 2 tbsp cilantro, chopped

METHOD

  1. Dice the onion, mince the garlic and grate the ginger.
  2. Cook the onion in the oil for 8 minutes, until golden.Done when: golden.
    TIMER 8 MINMEDIUM HEAT
  3. Add the garlic, ginger, cumin, coriander, turmeric and chili powder for 1 minute.
    TIMER 1 MINMEDIUM HEAT
  4. Add the tomatoes and cook for 5 minutes.
    TIMER 5 MINMEDIUM HEAT
  5. Add the chickpeas, water and salt. Simmer for 15 minutes.Done when: thick gravy.
    TIMER 15 MINLOW HEAT
  6. Stir in the garam masala and lemon juice, and top with cilantro.
