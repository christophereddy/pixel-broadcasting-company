Café de Olla

Mexican coffee brewed with cinnamon and brown sugar.

Drink · Mexico

EASYVEGETARIAN

AT A GLANCE

15 mintotal time
2 minprep
13 mincooking
4servings

Good for: breakfast, brunch.

Contains: none of the major allergens.

INGREDIENTS

  • 4 cups water
  • 1 cinnamon (stick)
  • ¼ cup brown sugar
  • 1 orange, zested (a strip of peel)
  • ¼ cup ground coffee (coarse)

METHOD

  1. Bring the water, cinnamon, brown sugar and orange peel to a boil.
    HIGH HEAT
  2. Simmer for 5 minutes.
    TIMER 5 MINLOW HEAT
  3. Stir in the coffee, take it off the heat and steep for 5 minutes.
    TIMER 5 MIN
  4. Strain into mugs.
