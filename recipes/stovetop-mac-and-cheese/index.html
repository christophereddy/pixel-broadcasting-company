Stovetop Mac and Cheese

Creamy cheddar mac and cheese made in one pot.

Main · American

EASYKID-FRIENDLYVEGETARIAN

AT A GLANCE

25 mintotal time
5 minprep
20 mincooking
4servings

Good for: weeknight dinner, comfort food.

Contains: Milk, Wheat.

INGREDIENTS

  • 8 oz elbow pasta
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups milk
  • 2 cups cheddar, shredded
  • ½ tsp mustard powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 8 cups water

METHOD

  1. Bring the water and 1/2 tsp salt to a boil.
    HIGH HEATGROWN-UP HELPS
  2. Cook the pasta for 7 minutes, then drain.Done when: tender with a little bite.
    TIMER 7 MINHIGH HEATGROWN-UP HELPS
  3. Melt the butter in the pot, stir in the flour and cook for 1 minute.
    TIMER 1 MINMEDIUM HEATGROWN-UP HELPS
  4. Whisk in the milk a little at a time and cook for 4 minutes, until thick.Done when: coats the spoon.
    TIMER 4 MINMEDIUM HEATGROWN-UP HELPS
  5. Take off the heat and stir in the cheddar, mustard powder and the rest of the salt.
  6. Stir the pasta back in.
