Chicken Tacos

Spiced chicken thighs in warm tortillas.

Main · Mexican-American

EASY

AT A GLANCE

30 mintotal time
10 minprep
20 mincooking
4servings

Good for: weeknight dinner, party.

Contains: Milk, Wheat.

INGREDIENTS

  • 1½ lb chicken thighs (boneless, skinless)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • 8 flour tortillas (small)
  • 2 cups lettuce, shredded
  • 1 cup cheddar, shredded
  • ½ cup sour cream
  • ¼ cup cilantro, chopped

METHOD

  1. Rub the chicken with the chili powder, cumin, garlic powder and salt.
  2. Heat the oil in a pan and cook the chicken for 6 minutes a side.
    TIMER 6 MINMEDIUM-HIGH HEAT
  3. Check the thickest piece: it should read 165°F inside.Done when: no pink inside.
    MEDIUM-HIGH HEATCHECK 165°F / 74°C INSIDE
  4. Rest the chicken for 5 minutes, then slice it.
    TIMER 5 MIN
  5. Squeeze the lime over the chicken.
  6. Warm the tortillas in a dry pan, 30 seconds a side.
    TIMER 30 SECMEDIUM HEAT
  7. Fill with chicken, lettuce, cheddar, sour cream and cilantro.
