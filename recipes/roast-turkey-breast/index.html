Herb Roast Turkey Breast

A juicy bone-in turkey breast with sage butter.

Main · American

HARD

AT A GLANCE

2 hr 15 mintotal time
15 minprep
1 hr 45 mincooking
6servings

Good for: holiday, family dinner.

Contains: Milk.

INGREDIENTS

  • 5 lb turkey breast (bone-in)
  • 4 tbsp butter, softened
  • 6 leaves sage, chopped
  • 4 sprigs thyme, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper

METHOD

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
    OVEN 350°F / 177°C
  2. Chop the sage and thyme leaves and mince the garlic. Mash them into the butter with the salt and pepper.
  3. Pat the turkey dry and rub the butter under and over the skin.
  4. Roast for 105 minutes, basting halfway.Done when: golden skin.
    TIMER 105 MINOVEN 350°F / 177°C
  5. Check the thickest part: 165°F.
    OVEN 350°F / 177°CCHECK 165°F / 74°C INSIDE
  6. Rest for 20 minutes, then carve.
    TIMER 20 MIN
