The Day-After Turkey Sandwich

Leftover turkey, cranberry and Swiss on sourdough.

Main · American

EASYQUICK

AT A GLANCE

10 mintotal time
10 minprep
0 mincooking
2servings

Good for: leftovers, lunch.

Contains: Milk, Egg, Wheat.

INGREDIENTS

  • 4 slices sourdough bread, toasted
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 6 oz leftover roast turkey, sliced
  • 4 tbsp cranberry sauce
  • 2 slices swiss cheese, sliced
  • 2 leaves lettuce

METHOD

  1. Toast the sourdough.
  2. Spread mayonnaise on 2 slices and cranberry sauce on the other 2.
  3. Layer the turkey, Swiss and lettuce.
  4. Close the sandwiches and cut them in half.
