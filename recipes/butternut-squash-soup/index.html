Butternut Squash Soup

Velvety roasted squash soup with a hint of nutmeg.

Main · American

MEDIUMVEGETARIAN

AT A GLANCE

1 hrtotal time
15 minprep
45 mincooking
4servings

Good for: weeknight dinner, comfort food.

Contains: Milk.

INGREDIENTS

  • 1 butternut squash, cubed (about 3 lb)
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 4 cups vegetable stock
  • ½ cup heavy cream
  • ¼ tsp nutmeg
  • 1 tsp salt
  • Black pepper, to taste

METHOD

  1. Preheat the oven to 400°F.
    OVEN 400°F / 204°C
  2. Peel the squash, scoop out the seeds and cut it into cubes.
  3. Toss the squash with 1 tbsp oil and roast for 30 minutes.Done when: soft and caramelised.
    TIMER 30 MINOVEN 400°F / 204°C
  4. Chop the onion and mince the garlic.
  5. Cook the onion and garlic in the rest of the oil for 5 minutes.
    TIMER 5 MINMEDIUM HEAT
  6. Add the squash and stock and simmer for 10 minutes.
    TIMER 10 MINMEDIUM-LOW HEAT
  7. Blend until smooth, then stir in the cream, nutmeg, salt and pepper.
