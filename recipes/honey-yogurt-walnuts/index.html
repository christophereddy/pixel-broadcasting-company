Greek Yogurt with Honey and Walnuts

Thick yogurt with warm honey and toasted walnuts.

Dessert · Greece

EASYQUICKVEGETARIAN

AT A GLANCE

8 mintotal time
5 minprep
3 mincooking
4servings

Good for: party, snack, breakfast.

Contains: Milk, Tree nuts.

INGREDIENTS

  • 2 cups greek yogurt, cold
  • ½ cup walnuts, toasted
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 1 pinch cinnamon

METHOD

  1. Toast the walnuts in a dry pan for 3 minutes.Done when: fragrant.
    TIMER 3 MINMEDIUM HEAT
  2. Spoon the yogurt into bowls.
  3. Top with walnuts, a drizzle of honey and a pinch of cinnamon.
