Beef and Bean Chili

Hearty beef chili with kidney beans and all the toppings.

Main · American

MEDIUM

AT A GLANCE

1 hr 15 mintotal time
15 minprep
1 hrcooking
6servings

Good for: game day, comfort food.

Contains: Milk.

INGREDIENTS

  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1½ lb ground beef
  • 3 tbsp chili powder
  • 2 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1½ tsp salt
  • 1 can canned crushed tomatoes (28 oz can)
  • 2 cans canned kidney beans, drained (15 oz cans)
  • 1 cup beef stock
  • 1 cup cheddar, shredded
  • ½ cup sour cream

METHOD

  1. Dice the onion and pepper and mince the garlic.
  2. Cook them in the oil for 5 minutes.
    TIMER 5 MINMEDIUM HEAT
  3. Add the beef and brown it for 8 minutes, breaking it up.Done when: no pink left.
    TIMER 8 MINMEDIUM-HIGH HEAT
  4. Stir in the chili powder, cumin, paprika and salt for 1 minute.
    TIMER 1 MINMEDIUM HEAT
  5. Add the tomatoes, beans and stock. Simmer for 45 minutes, stirring now and then.Done when: thick.
    TIMER 45 MINLOW HEAT
  6. Check it reads 160°F, then serve topped with cheddar and sour cream.
    CHECK 160°F / 71°C INSIDE
