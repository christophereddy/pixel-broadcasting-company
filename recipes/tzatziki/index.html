Tzatziki

Cool Greek yogurt sauce with cucumber, garlic and dill.

Sauce · Greece

EASYQUICKVEGETARIAN

AT A GLANCE

10 mintotal time
10 minprep
0 mincooking
6servings

Good for: party, picnic, weeknight dinner.

Contains: Milk.

INGREDIENTS

  • 1 cucumber, grated
  • 1½ cups greek yogurt, cold
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp dill, chopped
  • ½ lemon, juiced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • ½ tsp salt

METHOD

  1. Grate the cucumber and squeeze out the water.
  2. Mince the garlic and chop the dill.
  3. Stir everything into the yogurt with the lemon and salt.
  4. Drizzle with olive oil.
