Croque Monsieur

A toasted ham and Gruyère sandwich under creamy béchamel.

Main · France

MEDIUM

AT A GLANCE

35 mintotal time
15 minprep
20 mincooking
4servings

Good for: lunch, brunch.

Contains: Milk, Wheat.

INGREDIENTS

  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 pinch nutmeg
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • 8 slices sandwich bread
  • 2 tbsp dijon mustard
  • 8 slices sliced ham, sliced
  • 1½ cups gruyère, grated

METHOD

  1. Preheat the oven to 425°F.
    OVEN 425°F / 218°C
  2. Melt the butter, stir in the flour, then whisk in the milk for 4 minutes, until thick.Done when: thick sauce.
    TIMER 4 MINMEDIUM HEAT
  3. Stir in the nutmeg, salt and 1/2 cup Gruyère.
  4. Spread mustard on 4 slices of bread, add 2 slices of ham each, then close the sandwiches.
  5. Spoon the sauce over the tops and scatter on the rest of the Gruyère.
  6. Bake for 12 minutes, until bubbling and browned.Done when: bubbling, browned.
    TIMER 12 MINOVEN 425°F / 218°C
