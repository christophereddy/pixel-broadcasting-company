Grilled Cheese Croutons

Little grilled cheese squares to float on soup.

Side · American

EASYKID-FRIENDLYVEGETARIAN

AT A GLANCE

15 mintotal time
5 minprep
8 mincooking
4servings

Good for: comfort food, weeknight dinner.

Contains: Milk, Wheat.

INGREDIENTS

  • 4 slices sandwich bread
  • 2 tbsp butter, softened
  • 4 slices cheddar, sliced

METHOD

  1. Butter one side of each slice of bread.
    GROWN-UP HELPS
  2. Make 2 sandwiches with the cheddar inside and the butter outside.
  3. Cook for 4 minutes a side, until golden and melted.Done when: golden, cheese melted.
    TIMER 4 MINMEDIUM HEATGROWN-UP HELPS
  4. Cut each sandwich into 16 small squares.
    GROWN-UP HELPS
