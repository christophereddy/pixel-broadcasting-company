Mummy Hot Dogs

Hot dogs wrapped in puff pastry bandages with mustard eyes.

Snack · American

EASYKID-FRIENDLY

AT A GLANCE

35 mintotal time
15 minprep
18 mincooking
8servings

Good for: party, holiday.

Contains: Milk, Egg, Wheat.

INGREDIENTS

  • 1 sheet puff pastry (thawed)
  • 8 hot dogs
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 tbsp yellow mustard

METHOD

  1. Preheat the oven to 400°F.
    OVEN 400°F / 204°CGROWN-UP HELPS
  2. Cut the pastry into thin strips with a pizza cutter.
    GROWN-UP HELPS
  3. Wrap the strips around each hot dog like bandages, leaving a gap for the face.
  4. Brush the pastry with beaten egg.
  5. Bake for 18 minutes, until puffed and golden.Done when: puffed and golden.
    TIMER 18 MINOVEN 400°F / 204°CGROWN-UP HELPS
  6. Dot on 2 mustard eyes.
