Garlic Green Beans

Snappy green beans with garlic butter and almonds.

Side · American

EASYVEGETARIAN

AT A GLANCE

15 mintotal time
5 minprep
10 mincooking
4servings

Good for: family dinner, holiday.

Contains: Milk, Tree nuts.

INGREDIENTS

  • 1 lb green beans (trimmed)
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 cloves garlic, sliced
  • ¼ cup sliced almonds, toasted
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 8 cups water

METHOD

  1. Boil the green beans in the water for 4 minutes, then drain.Done when: bright green, still crisp.
    TIMER 4 MINHIGH HEAT
  2. Slice the garlic.
  3. Melt the butter in a pan and cook the garlic for 1 minute.
    TIMER 1 MINMEDIUM HEAT
  4. Toss in the beans, almonds and salt.
    MEDIUM HEAT
