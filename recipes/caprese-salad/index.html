Caprese Salad

Tomato, fresh mozzarella and basil with good olive oil.

Starter · Italy

EASYQUICKVEGETARIAN

AT A GLANCE

10 mintotal time
10 minprep
0 mincooking
4servings

Good for: family dinner, lunch, picnic.

Contains: Milk.

INGREDIENTS

  • 3 tomatoes, sliced
  • 8 oz fresh mozzarella, sliced
  • 12 leaves basil
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • ½ tsp salt
  • Black pepper, to taste

METHOD

  1. Slice the tomatoes and mozzarella.
  2. Overlap them on a plate with a basil leaf between each.
  3. Drizzle with olive oil and season with salt and pepper.
