My Kitchen

Write down a recipe of your own, and the PBC Cooking kitchen will make it for you, step by step.

Pick your ingredients, write the steps, and say what goes in at each one. The recipe check below makes sure the amounts add up and the timers, heat and temperatures are all there, the same check every PBC recipe passes. Then press WATCH IT MADE.

Your recipes are saved in this browser, on this device. Nothing is sent to PBC and nobody else can see them. Clearing your browser's data deletes them, so use DOWNLOAD to keep a copy.
