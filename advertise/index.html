QUESTIONS

DOT DELGADO Head of Sales

Hi, I'm Dot, head of sales. Pick a question and I'll answer it.

What kinds of ads do you sell?
Four kinds: arena boards in every sport, the timeout screen, presented-by segments on the News channel, and a spot on the newsroom set. They're all text, in our pixel lettering.
How much does it cost?
Rates aren't set yet. Send a request and we'll get back to you when booking opens.
When can my ad start?
Sponsorships open soon. Send a request now with the month you'd like to start, and you'll be near the front of the line.
Can I send a logo, video or banner ad?
Not yet. For now every ad is text, your business name and a short line, set in the channel's pixel lettering so it fits the world.
Can viewers get to my website?
Yes. If you give us your website, a QR code to it appears with your ad during timeouts and commercial breaks.
Will the cast talk about my product?
Only in your sponsor slot, with one fixed line you approve in advance. Never inside a news story.
Do sponsors affect the news?
No. Sponsors never choose or shape coverage. It's all in our Ad Policy.
Can I choose a city or a sport?
Yes. Tick the boxes in the request form and we'll tell you what's open.
Who reads my request?
A person at PBC. Requests never go on air and never go to the AI that writes the news.
Is the cat for sale?
No. The cat is not for sale, and neither is the cat's nap schedule.
