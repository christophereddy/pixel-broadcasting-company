QUESTIONS

DOT DELGADO Head of Sales

Hi, I'm Dot, head of sales. Pick a question and I'll answer it.

What kinds of ads do you sell?
Two. In sports, your brand goes up on the arena boards and takes over the big screen during timeouts. In the news, I step up to our big screen during the commercial break and read your ad myself.
How much does it cost?
We haven't set rates yet. Send me a request and I'll get back to you as soon as booking opens.
When can my ad start?
Soon. Send a request with the month you'd like to start, and I'll put you near the front of the line.
Can I send a logo, video or banner ad?
Not yet. Right now every ad is text: your business name and a short line, in our pixel lettering so it fits right in.
Can viewers get to my website?
Yes. Give us your website and we'll show a QR code to it with your ad, in sports breaks and in news commercial breaks.
Will the cast talk about my product?
Only during your ad. The play-by-play announcer or I will read one line you've approved ahead of time. Nobody mentions you inside a news story.
Do sponsors affect the news?
No. Sponsors never pick or shape our coverage. The details are in our Ad Policy.
Can I choose a city or a sport?
Yes. In the request form, tick the sports you want, or tick a city desk and pick the city. I'll let you know what's open.
Who reads my request?
A real person here at PBC. Requests never go on air, and they never go to the AI that writes the news.
Is Batty the cat for sale?
Nope. Batty isn't for sale, and neither is Batty's nap schedule.
